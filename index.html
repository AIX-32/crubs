Getting Started

Click tiles on your farm to perform actions with the selected tool. Start with carrot seeds and work your way up!

Tools

H - Harvest crops
W - Water plots
I - Info on hover
P - Plant seeds
D - Delete crop
B - Bonemeal (boost)

Upgrades

Tool Shop unlocks area watering, area harvesting, and bonemeal.

Water

Water level affects growth speed. Below 4 stops growth.

Crops

Stages: . (seed) → + (growing) → # (ready). P = perennial, X = exotic.

Black Market

Sell 10 of the daily special crop for 10x normal price.
