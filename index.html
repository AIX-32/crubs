> HELP

GETTING STARTED

Select a tool, then click tiles on your farm. Start with carrot seeds — buy more from the seed shop.

TOOLS

H — Harvest ready crops
W — Water a plot
I — Hover for info
P — Plant selected seed
D — Remove a crop
B — Bonemeal (10¢)

CROP DISPLAY

Each tile shows: NAME + STAGE + WATER
Stages: . sprouting → + growing → # ready
Water shown as 0–9 (9 = full). P = perennial. X = exotic.

WATER

Crops below 4 water stop growing. Rain auto-waters all plots to at least 7. Sunny weather gives a 20% growth boost.

UPGRADES (SHOP)

Water bucket upgrade: waters a 3×3 area. Harvest upgrade: harvests 3×3. Bonemeal: +30% instant progress per use.

BLACK MARKET

A new crop is chosen each day. Sell 10 of it for 10× the normal price.

COMPANION PLANTING

Corn (CRN) + Bean (BEN) planted adjacent grow 20% faster.

EXOTIC PLANTS

Roll for 200 coins. Slow growing but high value. Rarer = lower chance.

STOCK MARKET

Pick a crop and watch the simulated price chart. Hit BUY/SELL to trade at the shown integer price.

BEETROOT BOSS

The beetroot boss cycles through 20 random requests before demanding 10 UZZY seeds. Feeding it consumes crops but unlocks an achievement.

FARM CALCULATOR

Compare crop sell value vs growth speed, plug in custom crops, and use the speed/cost calculator to see how long until a purchase pays back.
