> HELP

How to Play: Click tiles on your farm to perform actions based on the selected tool.

Tools:
H - Harvest mature crops (#)
W - Water a plot (increases water level)
I - Info (shows growth progress on hover)
P - Plant seeds (uses selected seed)
D - Delete a crop
B - Bonemeal (costs 10 coins, boosts growth)

Upgrades: Visit the Tool Shop to unlock area watering, area harvesting, and the bonemeal tool.

Water: Watering crops makes them grow faster, if the water level goes under 4 it will stop growing.

Crops: Seeds take time to grow through stages (. + #). Perennials (*P*) produce repeatedly after harvest.

Exotic Plants: Rare plants obtained by rolling in the Exotic modal (200 coins). They grow slowly but need little water.
